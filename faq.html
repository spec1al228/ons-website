Your questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

The essentials about our courses and how to choose your preparation.

Which exams do you prepare students for?

We offer preparation for OMPT-F and OMPT-D. We are not currently offering a CCVX course.

How do I know which OMPT variant I need?

Your university programme determines the required variant and minimum score. Check its official admissions information before choosing a course. Do not assume that a different variant can be used instead.

Are the lessons in English?

Yes. All lessons are taught in English and are intended for international university applicants.

Are these individual or group lessons?

Our courses are live online group lessons. The group size and session dates will be confirmed before booking.

How long is a course?

Each course includes 12 hours of live teaching, split into three four-hour sessions on separate days. The detailed timetable and breaks will be confirmed before booking.

What happens during the lessons?

We combine theory with worked examples and plenty of problem-solving, including exam-style questions and mock-test practice.

Will there be homework?

Yes, a small amount of homework will help you practise between sessions.

Can I access the materials afterwards?

Yes. Lesson presentations and practice files will be available after the lessons. Video recordings are not currently included in the stated course offering.

How much does a course cost?

The current indicative price is €120 for a 12-hour course. The final price and other booking details will be confirmed before you enrol.

Does the course include the official exam?

ONS provides preparation lessons, not the official OMPT exam. Check the official exam provider’s information for exam registration and fees.

Do you guarantee a passing score?

No. Results depend on your starting level, practice and performance on the exam. Our courses support preparation but do not guarantee a score or university admission.
